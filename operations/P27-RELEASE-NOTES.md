# Gomoku 1.0.0

Gomoku 1.0.0 is the first stable release of the complete Gomoku Studio experience.

## Included

- **Play** — local play, AI opponents, online rooms, ranked matchmaking, match history and player identity.
- **Rules** — standard Gomoku plus Renju legality and forbidden-move enforcement.
- **Improve** — structured learning content and guided practice.
- **Analysis** — move evaluation, forcing-defense analysis, mistake detection and post-game analysis.
- **Guided Review** — a dedicated review workspace for finding critical moments and understanding better alternatives.
- **Competitive systems** — ratings, seasons, tournaments, profiles and supporting integrity controls.
- **Offline/PWA** — installable app behavior, local persistence, offline recovery and reconnect handling.
- **Accessibility and responsive UX** — keyboard navigation, target-size checks, focus visibility, high-contrast/large-text support and mobile qualification.

## Release quality

The stable candidate passed the repository's automated correctness, security, recovery, capacity, browser/PWA and product-quality suites. Stable release additionally requires the P26 physical Android qualification and burn-in gate.

After 1.0, Gomoku moves to maintenance mode. New work should be driven by reproduced defects or clearly justified product improvements rather than continuing phase expansion.
